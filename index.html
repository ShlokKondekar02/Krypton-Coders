WHO WE ARE

Built by Students, Growing Together

Krypton Coders is a community of campus learners, hobbyists, and aspiring software developers. We believe the best way to master coding is by building fun projects and learning alongside friends.

Peer-to-Peer Learning

Weekend study jams, beginner roadmaps, and senior mentorship to help you get started with programming without feeling overwhelmed.

  • Weekly campus meetups
  • Senior student advice & project feedback

Hackathons & Contests

Forming campus squads to participate in college hackathons, friendly speed-coding scrims, and regional coding competitions.

  • Friendly algorithmic quizzes
  • First-time hackathon team matching

Hands-on Projects

Moving beyond textbook theory by building interactive websites, utility tools, campus bots, and personal portfolio showcases.

  • Team project collaborations
  • GitHub & version control practice
