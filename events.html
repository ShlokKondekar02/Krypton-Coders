SCHEDULE BREAKDOWN

24-Hour Hackathon Timeline

Here is what a typical Krypton Coders campus hackathon weekend looks like from start to finish.

Saturday • 09:30 AM

Check-in & Team Registration

Participants arrive at the college CS lab, set up their workstations, connect to high-speed campus Wi-Fi, and grab club badges.

Saturday • 10:30 AM

Problem Statement Reveal & Hacking Begins

Themes around campus utilities, sustainability, educational tools, and open innovation are announced. Teams start planning and building.

Saturday • 05:00 PM

Mentor Review Round #1

Senior student developers and alumni mentors visit each team's table to debug roadblocks, suggest libraries, and refine project architecture.

Sunday • 12:00 AM (Midnight)

Midnight Pizza & Mini Gaming Break

Snacks, tea, and quick multiplayer games (Skribbl.io & typing races) to recharge everyone's energy for the overnight coding sprint.

Sunday • 10:30 AM

Code Freeze & Live Demos

Teams push their code to GitHub, deploy demo links, and present a 3-minute live demonstration to student peers and faculty judges.
